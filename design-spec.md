# Road to the Championship — current design

One high-school basketball season: preseason recruiting, 10 regular-season games, then up to four playoff rounds. The goal is the State Championship in that season. Replays start fresh; there is no offseason or career advancement.

The signature experience is **What if, Coach? — Decision Replay**. A player reviews a completed matchup, changes tactics and compares controlled simulation branches without spending resources or modifying the actual outcome. An exhibition on the title screen makes the experience accessible immediately.

The visual system pairs collegiate typography with original basketball equipment illustrations, school-colored jerseys and the live Canvas court. SVG gradients and texture patterns provide basketball/trophy detail without external image dependencies. Motion follows the existing reduced-motion preference.

The player journey is title → school setup/tryouts (or Quick Start) → once-per-browser tutorial → practice → off-court challenge → tactics/live game → recap/Decision Replay → next week → final season outcome. Player cards support saved first/last-name edits. Credits and sources remain available during play.

## M0: baseline and instrumentation (no behaviour change)

Verified in the code (the audit holds): sampleClip (src/basketball.js) places every player by authored waypoints, a
Catmull-Rom route for the shooter run on a `0.35 m + 0.65 smoothstep(m)` curve, offBallV2's ease-in-out hops between holds
(version 2) or a sine sway (version 1), defenders on guardSpot with sines added; the ball is analytic (fly/launch, a
dribble solved from gravity, a sine-arc pass, a scripted carom), placed for the old 0.28 ball (floor contact 0.3; the drawn
ball is 0.13 and Arena.settleBall drops it near the floor); the Arena guesses the holder (nearest within 0.85) and pushes
players apart frame by frame (motion.js keepApart). A sample costs about 21 µs.

Tools (all deterministic, no game code changed):

- `scripts/motion-lib.mjs`: the clip set (every shot kind x outcome (made, miss, block won by the defence, block with a
  made or missed putback, steal) x contest x team x lane = 936, a seeded quarter as the play after a steal, plus 96 plays
  of four seeded engine games: 1,032 clips), 60 Hz sampling, the anchors, the metrics. **Decision:** 1 unit = 0.929 m
  (BALL: 1 ft = 0.328 units), not the brief's 0.3306 estimate.
- `npm run motion:qa` (`scripts/motion-qa.mjs`): samples every clip as version 1, 2 and the current version. Always: no
  NaN, determinism (the current version sampled backwards in time gives the same frames), versions 1 and 2 bit-identical
  to `docs/motion/qa-baseline.json` (frozen now; `--freeze` refuses to change an existing hash). For version 3 on: caps
  (speed 9.5 m/s, acceleration 12 m/s² and jerk 900 m/s³ at the 99.9th percentile), no overlap (SPACE, or a contact the
  pose declares), the ball never jumping more than 0.45 in a frame, never below the floor or through the board, within
  0.26 of the hand holding it, bounces keeping 0.78–0.92 of their speed, and the anchors (phases, resolved and flying
  frames, release spot, ball at release, rim, block and end) as in version 2. 1,032 clips in 12 s.
- `npm run motion:render` (`scripts/motion-render.mjs`): per play family (8) and four outcome cases (a miss, a block with
  a putback, a steal, the visitors' catch-and-shoot): top-down paths coloured by speed, an eight-moment strip with
  velocity arrows, and ball height / ball-to-hand / player-speed plots. **Before** set: `docs/motion/before/` (36 images).

Critique of the before pictures (drive shown; the others agree):

- Paths: straight segments joined at corners (the off-ball men's ease-in-out hops), the help defender a dead-straight line
  at 9 m/s, the on-ball defender a sine zig-zag. Fails "paths curve naturally" and "cuts show a plant".
- Speed: the shooter's run is a smooth bell (fine) but every off-ball hop is 0 → peak → 0 with a cusp at each stop (jerk
  spikes, p99.9 8,000 m/s³), peaks to 9–13.5 m/s for a cut (too fast), defenders starting at speed on frame 0 and dropping
  in steps. Fails "visible acceleration and braking".
- Spacing: pairs on top of each other at the start (a defender on his man's guard spot), five players crowding the rim at
  the end; 247 overlapping pair-frames per clip on average (the Arena's push-apart hides them). Fails "nobody overlaps".
- Ball: the dribble is physical but for the old 0.28 ball (floor contact 0.3); nothing says who holds it; the pass is a
  sine arc; the miss is a single carom to the rebound spot.

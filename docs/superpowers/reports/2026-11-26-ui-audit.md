# KANGS · Road to the Championship: UI/UX audit (2026-11-26)

Review mode: OilOil UI/UX guide, `review` mode with its ten UX Hard Rules. The game is judged against its own rules
(`CLAUDE.md`, `src/ui/README.md`): one purple primary button per screen that is the way forward, cards use secondary
buttons, headings are plain labels and things are said once, a disabled button says why next to it, colours come
from tokens, and the light theme and a 390 px phone are checked.

Findings marked **[J]** would likely be noticed by an FBLA judge in the first 5 minutes.

## Context

- **Surface:** a desktop-first web game that also runs on a phone. It has a small set of hub pages (Home, Prep,
  Game day, Roster, Shop tabs, Season tabs) around one real-time screen (the live 3D game).
- **Users:** FBLA judges and students. Most of them are first-time players with 5 to 15 minutes, and some are on a
  phone.
- **Primary task:** play one game week: prepare, set the five, tip off and play, then talk to the press. Spending
  tokens in the Shop is the secondary loop.
- **Primary CTA:** the engine's `nextStep()` rendered as the purple button (`nextStepButton()` in
  `src/ui/screens/home.js`).
- **Evidence:** the 128 PNGs in `ui-shots/` (all 16 pages read in desktop-dark-mid and phone-dark-mid, with spot
  checks of the light and pre saves), and `ui-shots/lessons/desk-*` and `phone-*` for the live game and the press
  room. The coach cards themselves were not reviewed. Every cause below was confirmed in the source unless it says
  "likely".

## Findings

### P0: blockers

**P0-1. Phone Game day: the scouting drawers cover the top bar [J on phone]**
- Evidence: `phone-dark-mid-game.png` and `phone-light-mid-game.png`. "Scouting report" and "Tip-off preview" are
  drawn at y≈12 px over the header, so the KANGS logo, Settings and Help are gone.
- Diagnosis: execution gulf (Settings and Help can't be reached), and a layout bug.
- Cause: `.court-drawers` is `position:absolute; top:12px` (`src/theme.css` ~L380). Its anchor,
  `body:not(.live-match)[data-view='game'] .court-panel { position: relative }`, only exists inside
  `@media (min-width: 761px)` (`theme.css` ~L1469). On a phone the drawers anchor to the page instead.
- Fix: move `position: relative` onto `body[data-view='game'] .court-panel` outside the media query. On phones it is
  also worth turning the two drawers into an in-flow row above the gym (`position: static`) instead of an overlay.
- Acceptance: in `npm run check:ui -- --shots`, `phone-*-game.png` shows the top bar unobstructed and both drawers
  sitting inside the gym frame.

**P0-2. Game day offers "Tip off" as the primary while the next step is something else, and tipping off silently
throws work away [J]**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-home.png` says "Next step: Talk to the press." with the purple "Open the press room".
  `desktop-dark-mid-game.png` shows "Ready for tip-off?" with a purple **Tip off**. In the pre save, Home says "Open
  preseason", but Game day still offers Tip off as the primary.
- Diagnosis: mistake (two conflicting "ways forward" break the conceptual model), and an evaluation gulf (nothing
  tells you what was lost).
- Cause: `game()` in `src/ui/screens/game.js` always renders `btn(t('game.tipOff'), 'start-match', 'primary wide')`.
  `E.startMatch()` (`engine.js` ~L655) then sets `s.press.done = true` (the press is skipped without the −10 fans that
  "Skip" costs), calls `endBreak(s)` (closes an open preseason or midseason break), and the week's unused prep slots
  are reset to 3 at the end of the game (`engine.js` ~L1139) without ever having been used.
- Fix: in the rail of `game.js`, when `E.nextStep(state).id !== 'tipoff'`, render `nextStepButton('primary wide')`
  as the primary. Show Tip off as `secondary wide`, with one line under it listing what will be skipped, built from
  state, for example "3 prep slots unused · press room not done". For the press, either keep that line or make Tip
  off apply the same penalty as `press-skip`, so the two paths agree.
- Acceptance: with a pending press, an open break or unused slots, Game day shows exactly one purple button and it
  matches Home's. Tip off is secondary and states the cost. With a clean week, Tip off is the only primary.

### P1: important

**P1-1. Locked achievements can't be read [J]**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-standings.png` and `desktop-light-mid-standings.png`. Five of seven achievements carry a
  large red LOCKED stamp over their goal text, and the text is at 32 % opacity. "Get through the regular season with
  no injuries" is unreadable. In light mode it is grey on white at roughly 1.5:1.
- Diagnosis: evaluation gulf. The useful part (how to earn it) is the part that is hidden.
- Cause: `src/style.css` ~L6436–6455: `.ach-list li.locked > * { opacity: .32 }` plus `::after` with `inset: 0` and a
  stamp image centred at 76 px.
- Fix: reuse the `.kit.locked::after` pattern, a small stamp in the top-right corner (about 72×38 px, `inset: auto`,
  `right: 8px`, `top: 8px`). Raise the text to `opacity: .7` (or use `color: var(--ink-2)` with no opacity), and keep
  `.ach-state` visible.
- Acceptance: `check:ui` reports no low-contrast nodes inside `.ach-list`, and each goal sentence and its "1 / 13"
  progress is readable in both themes.

**P1-2. Title screen: an empty grey band above the gym in the save state [J]**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-start-form.png`, `desktop-dark-pre-start-form.png` and
  `desktop-light-pre-start-form.png`. The top 280 px of the right half is flat grey and the scoreboard is cut by a hard
  edge. Without a save (`lessons/desk-start-form.png`) only the 4vh letterbox shows. In the same state, "Continue
  season" and "Take over the Kangs" are both purple once "New season" has opened the form, and the "Judge demo" link
  is pushed below the fold.
- Diagnosis: first-impression polish, plus two primaries (Hard Rule 1).
- Cause: likely that `.landing-visual` / `#arena` (`style.css` ~L6497–6511) is sized to the `.landing` box. When the
  form column grows past 100dvh, the canvas no longer fills the area. Verify in `npm run dev`.
- Fix: make `.landing-visual` `position: fixed; inset: 0` so the gym stays viewport-sized (or resize the renderer
  when `#arena` resizes). In `landing.js`, once `#start-form` is shown, render Continue as `secondary` so "Take over
  the Kangs" is the only primary. Move "Judge demo" up beside Continue/New season when a save exists.
- Acceptance: no grey band at 1440×900 with a save. With the form open, exactly one `.primary` is in `.landing-copy`.

**P1-3. Purple means both "primary action" and "selected", so screens show several purple buttons [J]**
- Evidence:
  - `desktop-dark-mid-data.png`: "Junior Varsity" is a filled purple button.
  - `desktop-dark-mid-gear.png`: "All gear" is filled purple next to the top nav's purple "Shop".
  - `desktop-dark-mid-help-lessons.png`: the active pill "Lessons" is purple.
  - The live game: the "Auto" camera and the "1×" speed are purple, and so is Watch live.
  - Prep (`prep.js`): every row in the plan becomes `primary small` ("✓ In plan · remove"), so a full plan shows
    four purple buttons with "Run the week".
- Diagnosis: violates the project's "one purple button" rule and Hard Rule 6 (same look, different meaning).
- Cause: the `[aria-pressed='true']` fills use `var(--accent)` in `theme.css` L609 (`.gear-filters`) and L1422
  (`.level-tabs`); the active `.nav-item` and the camera/speed buttons in `style.css` do the same; `prep.js`
  `row()` uses `on ? 'primary small' : 'secondary small'`.
- Fix: add a selected-state token pair in `theme.css`, for example `--selected-bg: var(--accent-soft)` and
  `--selected-ink: var(--accent-ink)` with a 2 px `var(--accent)` border or underline. Use it for every
  `aria-pressed` / `aria-current` control (top nav, sub-tabs already underline, level tabs, gear filters, help pills,
  camera, speed). In `prep.js`, keep plan rows `secondary`, with a check icon and `.prep-row.on` highlighting the row.
- Acceptance: on every captured page, `document.querySelectorAll('.primary:not(:disabled)')` plus anything else with
  an `--accent` fill counts to 1.

**P1-4. Phone: the primary action is far below the fold on the two core pages**
- Evidence:
  - `phone-dark-mid-practice.png`: "Run the week" is at y≈1,270 px and the plan slots at ≈1,010 px, so tapping "Add
    to plan" changes nothing visible on screen.
  - `phone-dark-mid-game.png`: "Tip off" is at y≈1,075 px (the viewport is 844 px), and the starting five slides
    under the fixed bottom nav.
- Diagnosis: execution gulf (Fitts, and the primary is not visible within 3 s) and evaluation gulf (no feedback in
  view after "Add to plan").
- Fix: in the `@media (max-width: 620px)` block of `theme.css`, make the page's action area a sticky bar above the tab
  bar: `.prep-side, body[data-view='game'] .right-rail .primary { position: sticky; bottom: calc(var(--tabh) + 8px);
  z-index: 20 }`. On Prep, collapse the three slots into a one-line summary in that bar ("2 of 3 picked · Run the
  week").
- Acceptance: on a 390×844 viewport, Run the week and Tip off are visible without scrolling. After "Add to plan", the
  pick count in the bar changes on screen.

**P1-5. Phone Shop: the token balance is hidden and two tabs are unreachable at a glance**
- Evidence: the cropped tops of `phone-dark-mid-recruit.png` and `phone-dark-mid-staff.png`. The sub-row shows
  "Tryouts · Staff · Skill tree", then the purple "Open the press room". Gear and Jerseys are off-screen with no
  scroll cue, and Program tokens appear nowhere on any Shop page, which is the one place they are spent.
- Diagnosis: execution gulf (hidden tabs) and knowledge in the head (the balance has to be remembered).
- Cause: `theme.css` ~L1534: `.status-strip { display: none }` on phones, except Team and Home.
  `subnav()` in `shell.js` puts the next-step button in the same row as the scrolling tabs.
- Fix: on phones, give `.subnav` a second row when `navGroup === 'shop'`, holding the strip with tokens only, and move
  the next-step button into the sticky action bar from P1-4. Add a right-edge fade (mask-image) to `.subnav nav` when
  it overflows.
- Acceptance: on `phone-*-recruit/staff/skills/gear/locker`, "Program tokens 1,162" is visible and all five tabs are
  visible or clearly scrollable.

**P1-6. Prep: "Run the week" is disabled with no reason, and the effect chips don't say good or bad**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-practice.png` and `desktop-light-mid-practice.png`. The disabled purple "Run the week"
  has nothing next to it, which breaks the project rule. The chips are all neutral grey and use mixed formats:
  "Energy -10", "−5 energy", "+180 tokens", "GPA -0.03". The press room, by contrast, uses green ▲ and red ▼ chips
  (`lessons/desk-press-room.png`). "Host a community clinic" has no description line, unlike every other row.
- Diagnosis: Hard Rules 3 and 6; an evaluation gulf about consequences.
- Fix:
  - In `prep.js` `practice()`, add `<small class="need-tokens">${t('prep.pickFirst')}</small>` under the disabled
    button ("Add at least one session").
  - Build `fx()` chips with the press room's chip helper (`chipHtml` in `markup.js`), which gives up/down colour and
    one order ("Energy −10", "Tokens +180").
  - Give `fundraise` a `prepDesc`.
  - Add the new key to all 7 locales with `scripts/i18n-set.mjs`.
- Acceptance: the disabled button has a visible reason, every chip reads "Stat ±n", and costs are red/▼ while gains
  are green/▲ in both themes.

**P1-7. Game day before tip-off: "Ready for tip-off?" gives nothing to judge readiness by [J]**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-game.png`. The rail lists five names and jersey numbers only. Opponent strength, style,
  energy and morale sit in two collapsed drawers ("Scouting report", "Tip-off preview") floating over the 3D
  scoreboard. The subtitle reads "Conference matchup · simulated outcome".
- Diagnosis: execution gulf. The decision ("is my five ready, do I need prep?") needs data that is hidden one level
  down.
- Fix: in `game.js`, put a compact line at the top of the rail: the opponent strength badge (already built:
  `strength-badge`), our average energy and the morale word, and the style if scouted, else "Not scouted · Opponent
  film" linking to Prep. Under each starter, show OVR and energy (reuse `.sq-energy`). Leave the drawers for the
  detail.
- Acceptance: without opening a drawer, a first-time player can say who is stronger and whether a starter is tired.

**P1-8. Standings: your own team is not marked, and the table has no columns [J]**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-standings.png`:
  - "1. Lake Washington 1–0" looks exactly like the other nine rows.
  - Six teams tie at 1–0 with no tiebreak shown.
  - There is no header row (W–L, Pct, Pts for/against, Streak).
  - The game calls the team "Kangs" everywhere else, so "Lake Washington" has to be mapped mentally.
  - Achievements and Season leaders share the page, so "Standings" holds three unrelated things.
- Diagnosis: scanning cost; concept constancy (Kangs vs Lake Washington).
- Cause: `standingsView()` in `season.js` renders `.staff-row` divs (a staff-list component) with only `w–l`.
- Fix: render a real `<table>` (`.table-wrap`, as in `boxTable`) with columns # · Team · W · L · Pct · Streak, and add
  `class="you"` on the user's row, with `--accent-soft` background and the label "Kangs (you)". Move achievements to
  the Journal page, or to their own sub-tab "Achievements" in `NAV.season`, and update `LESSONS` and `DEMO_VIEWS` if a
  route is renamed.
- Acceptance: the user's row is found in under 1 s, and the column headers are announced by a screen reader.

**P1-9. A tryout can't be compared with the current roster**
- Evidence:
  - `desktop-dark-mid-recruit.png`: prospects show "Skill 66 · Energy 90 · Sim. GPA 3".
  - `desktop-dark-mid-roster.png`: the roster shows only OVR (Karter Martin 65).
  - `desktop-dark-mid-gear.png`: the same player shows Skill 64.
  - Three different numbers, and no answer to "would this player start for me?".
- Diagnosis: mistake (different measures under similar names) and a cognitive load problem (the comparison has to be
  held in memory).
- Fix: in `breaks.js` `recruit()`, show the prospect's OVR (via `E.ratings`) as the big number, styled like
  `.sq-ovr`, and one comparison line ("Would be your 9th best · best Guard on the bench is 75"). Keep Skill in the
  detail chips. Use the label "Skill" consistently and explain OVR once in Help.
- Acceptance: each prospect card shows OVR on the same scale as the roster, plus one comparison line.

**P1-10. Live game: toasts cover the controls you need, and the play-by-play is closed by default**
- Evidence: `lessons/desk-tipoff-watch.png`. Two toasts ("Practice complete · 1 of 1 unlocked", "Achievement
  unlocked: Perfect Drill") cover Settings and Help and the "Play-by-play" and "Box score" drawers at tip-off. On
  phones, `#toast` sits at `inset: auto 12px 12px 12px` (`style.css` ~L2308), which is where Watch live, Next play and
  Timeout huddle are.
- Diagnosis: inattentional blindness and interruption cost at the most important moment.
- Fix: in `toast.js` `notify()`, while `document.body.classList.contains('live-match')`, queue non-error toasts and
  flush them at the next quarter break or in the result dialog. Errors stay immediate, placed at
  `body.live-match #toast { inset: 140px auto auto 24px }` (top left, under the score bug). Consider opening the
  play-by-play drawer by default on desktop, since the feed is the game's story.
- Acceptance: during Watch live, no toast overlaps `.court-drawers`, `.top-tools` or the rail buttons.

**P1-11. "Dismiss assistant" throws away a 300-token hire in one click**
- Evidence: in `actions.js`, `SEASON_ACTIONS.dismiss` sets `state.assistant = null` and logs "Assistant dismissed. No
  refund.", with no confirmation. `release` right above it does use `confirmDialog(... danger: true)`.
- Diagnosis: Hard Rule 4 (destructive and irreversible without deliberate confirmation); a slip risk because it sits
  in the heading row of the cards.
- Fix: wrap it in `confirmDialog(t('confirm.dismiss', { name, cost }), { yes: t('confirm.dismissYes'), danger: true
  })` and name the loss ("You lose the 300 tokens you paid").
- Acceptance: dismissing needs two deliberate clicks, and the dialog says what is lost.

**P1-12. The "way forward" moves around from page to page, and Season pages have none**
- Evidence: the next-step button appears in five different places:
  - Home: a large bar at the bottom right.
  - Roster: a toolbar button (`desktop-dark-mid-roster.png`).
  - Shop: a small button at the right end of the sub-row (`desktop-dark-mid-recruit.png`).
  - Prep: the side column.
  - Game day: the rail.
  - Journal, Bracket, Standings and Data have no forward action at all (`desktop-dark-mid-season.png`).
- Diagnosis: Hard Rule 6 (same action, different placement); the user loses the thread after browsing Season.
- Fix: give every non-Home page the same slot, the right end of `.subnav-end` (`shell.js` `subnav()`). Extend
  `subnavGo()` in `nav.js` to return `'next'` for the `season` group as well. Pages with their own primary (Prep,
  Game day) keep it and the slot is empty. On phones that slot becomes the sticky bar from P1-4.
- Acceptance: on every page the next step is in one predictable place, and `tests/nav.test.js` is extended to assert
  `subnavGo` for each group.

### P2: polish

**P2-1. Slogans as headings, and headings that say a thing twice**
- "Title run, win or go home" (`hb.g.run` on Bracket), "The campaign table" (under the "Standings" h1), "Bring it
  in." (timeout huddle), "Staff" › "Coaching staff", and "The real Kangs" (h1 of the tab "Real Kangs data").
- Fix: use plain labels: "Playoff bracket", "League table", "Timeout", "Assistant coach" / "Program upgrades", and
  match the h1 to the tab name. Locale keys: `hb.g.run`, `journal.table`, `huddle.title`, `data.title`.

**P2-2. The fiction/simulation disclaimer is repeated everywhere**
- It appears as "Fictional tryout ·" on each of the six cards, "Sim. GPA", "· simulated outcome" (Game day),
  "Simulated statistics from your season", "Fictional campaign standings… not official KingCo standings", "Tokens
  only, never real money" and "No athlete likeness is used."
- Fix: say it once, in the Data page intro and the Settings footer (which already has "Fictional simulation / sourced
  public roster"). Elsewhere use plain "Tryout · Guard" and "GPA".
- Locale keys: `recruit.tryout`, `roster.gpa`, `game.matchup`, `leaders.note`, `journal.tableNote`,
  `recruit.intro`, and the locker note.

**P2-3. The Journal's decision trail is hard to read**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-season.png`. Three entries in a row are each labelled "Game 1". The newest comes first,
  so the trail reads backwards. One line is "Budget: +202 income, −0 assistant, −40 travel (net 162)."
- Fix: in `journal()`, group `state.log` by `l.week` under one "Game 1 · vs Bothell" heading, oldest first within the
  game, and drop the zero terms from the budget line ("+202 income, −40 travel = +162 tokens").

**P2-4. Home timeline: it looks like a progress stepper with nothing done**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-home.png`. Four hollow dots ("Prepare for Redmond … Prepare for Juanita") sit under
  "Next step: Talk to the press.", and the "After that" label exists only in `aria-label`.
- Fix: in `home.js`, render a visible `<span class="eyebrow">${t('flow.later')}</span>` before `.home-path`, or start
  the line with the current step as a filled dot.

**P2-5. The spinning prospect jerseys catch edge-on**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-recruit.png` (Jordan Lee) and `desktop-light-mid-recruit.png` (Morgan Ellis). The card
  art shows a sliver, which looks broken.
- Fix: in `mountProspectJerseys` (`locker.js`), start every jersey facing front and rock it ±25° instead of a full
  spin. Alternatively, use the `avatar()` portrait the roster uses, so a signed player looks the same before and
  after signing.

**P2-6. Touch targets under 44 px on the phone**
- Live camera buttons are 26–28 px (`style.css` ~L4143: 12 px text, `6px 7px` padding). Speed buttons are 34 px
  (`.playback-controls button`). The Help chapter pills are about 28 px. The roster "Start" checkbox row is about
  20 px.
- Fix: `min-height: 44px` on `.camera-controls button`, `.playback-controls button` and the handbook pills under
  `@media (pointer: coarse)`. Make the whole `.sq-set label` the hit area, with `min-height: 44px; padding-inline:
  8px`.

**P2-7. Roster cards: energy is easy to miss and the captain can't be set from them**
- Evidence: `desktop-dark-mid-roster.png`. The starters' energy is 74 against 100 on the bench, shown in a 40 px bar of
  one colour. The "No captain yet" pill can't be clicked, though captaincy is an action, and there is no position
  balance cue (3 G / 2 F).
- Fix (`roster.js` `squadCard`):
  - Colour the bar with the `energyWord` thresholds, using `--good`, `--score` and `--bad` tokens.
  - Turn the pill into a button that opens Full stats on the best candidate.
  - Add "3 G · 2 F" to the toolbar.
- The Full stats table has no sorting: make the `<th>` elements buttons that sort by OVR, PPG and energy.

**P2-8. Gear: the hint points the wrong way, and buying is two steps**
- "Choose an owned item below" (`gear.pickBelow`) is shown, but owned items are to the right, behind the "Owned
  inventory" filter. Buying does not equip, so a second "Equip on …" click is needed. Each card is about 370 px tall,
  so only 3 items fit per screen.
- Fix:
  - Change the copy to "Buy or pick an owned item in the shop."
  - In `SEASON_ACTIONS['gear-buy']`, auto-equip on the shown player when the slot is empty.
  - Shrink `.gear-display` to about 96 px.

**P2-9. Locker copy is wrong for touch, and the unlock goals are hidden**
- `phone-dark-mid-locker.png`: the text says "Click a jersey…" and "press ← or → to change the player" on a phone.
- Fix: write the text without the input device ("Tap or click"), and show the keyboard tip only under
  `@media (hover: hover)`. Under each locked kit, print its unlock goal as a caption instead of waiting for a click.

**P2-10. Settings: where things live and what the badge means**
- The light theme sits under the "Game" tab (`desktop-dark-mid-settings-game.png`). The "Accessibility 2" badge means

- Fix: move the theme to Accessibility, or rename it to a "Display" tab. Give the badge an `aria-label` and a

**P2-11. Real Kangs data: dense intro, a heading that doesn't match, a stale date, and tables cut off**
- Evidence:
  - `desktop-dark-mid-data.png`: "Junior Varsity · 17 upcoming games" sits over a *player* table.

  - "Last successful source check: 10/24/2026", next to "The scheduled updater checks KingCo daily".

- Fix (`dataView()` in `season.js`):
  - Put "Roster" and "Schedule" headings over their tables.
  - Fold the second paragraph into a `<details>`.
  - Show "Checked 33 days ago" with a warning tone when the check is older than 2 days.
  - On phones, hide the Venue and Type columns, or add a right-edge fade on `.table-wrap`.

**P2-12. Live score bug: it overlaps the 3D scoreboard and the possession count is clipped**
- `lessons/desk-timeout-call.png`: the HTML bug sits on top of the 3D jumbotron, so the score shows twice, and "Poss
  12" under the clock is cut by the bug's edge.
- Fix: in `.bug-mid small`, give the line room (`line-height` and `padding-bottom`), or drop it. In Auto camera,

## What works well: keep it

- **Home's "Next step" bar** (`home.js`): a single, large, plainly worded primary ("Open the press room") over the
  gym. It is the strongest task-first moment in the game. Extend that discipline (P0-2, P1-12) rather than replace it.
- **The status strip** (Game · Record · Program tokens · Prep slots) on every desktop page uses tabular numbers in the
  score colour. Keep it, and bring it back on phone Shop pages.
- **Disabled-with-reason on Shop cards**: "Need 120 more tokens" (`need-tokens`), "Hired", and "Finish the game
  first". This is the project rule done right. Prep should copy it.
- **The press room and the timeout huddle state their consequences before you choose**: green ▲ and red ▼ chips, "+4
  energy", and "Skip the press conference (fans −10)". This is the model for Prep's chips.
- **Quarter break dialog**: a clean two-team stats table, one primary "Keep going" and a plain "No changes needed"
  line.
- **The break walk-through in the Shop**: the "Preseason" label in the sub-row, "Everything here is optional", "Next:
  Skill tree →" and a single "Start the season". The flow is clear and never locks anything.
- **Render keeps your place**: focus, scroll and open `<details>` survive every redraw (`shell.js`). Keyboard
  shortcuts (Space, T, 1–3, ?) cover the live game and the press room.
- **The token system**: colours come from tokens with a real light theme and high-contrast mode, and the type scales
  with `--ts`/`--ls`. Light mode holds up on most pages; the exceptions are locked achievements and the faint disabled
  purple.
- **The phone bottom tab bar** with icon and label, 4 items and a clear active state.
